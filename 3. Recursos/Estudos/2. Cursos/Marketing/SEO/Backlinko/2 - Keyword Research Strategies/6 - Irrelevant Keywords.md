### O que são palavras-chave irrelevantes?

Palavras-chave irrelevantes são consultas de pesquisa para as quais não faz sentido classificar seu site ou página da web. Esses termos de pesquisa não estão alinhados com seus objetivos de negócios ou com a finalidade do seu conteúdo.

Você pode classificar palavras-chave irrelevantes sem querer.

Por exemplo, a página de contato da marca de moda Coach é classificada para “coaching por e-mail”:

![Google SERP – Coach – Email coaching](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/12/google-serp-coach-email-coaching.png "Google SERP – Coach – Email coaching")

Ou você pode classificar palavras-chave irrelevantes intencionalmente.

Por exemplo, uma marca de moda pode criar uma postagem de blog para tentar aparecer para um termo popular como “fotos de gatos” Mesmo que não seja relevante para seu negócio principal, eles ainda podem se classificar para ele se tiverem autoridade suficiente [no site](https://backlinko.com/tools/website-authority "Free Website Authority Checker (Enter Any Domain or URL)").

### Como a classificação de palavras-chave irrelevantes pode prejudicar seu negócio

#### 1. Isso não gerará resultados comerciais

É improvável que classificações de palavras-chave irrelevantes gerem resultados para o seu negócio.

For example:

- Someone who searches “email coaching” is unlikely to click through to Coach’s contact page
- Someone who visits a fashion brand’s “cat pictures” blog post is unlikely to then buy some clothes

Don’t waste time creating [search-optimized content](https://backlinko.com/seo-content "SEO Content: How to Create Content That Ranks") around these keywords.

And don’t target irrelevant keywords through [pay-per-click advertising (PPC)](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-ppc/ "What Is PPC? A Starter Guide to Pay-Per-Click Marketing"), because you’ll still pay every time someone clicks your ad—whether they convert or (more likely) not.

#### 2. It May Indicate an Issue with Your Content

If your page ranks higher for irrelevant keywords than it does for relevant keywords, your page could fail to satisfy the [search intent](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/search-intent "Search Intent and SEO: A Complete Guide"). In other words, it doesn’t help the target user achieve their goal.

Irrelevant keyword rankings can also indicate that your page is poorly optimized. Google and users may not understand what it’s about.

If your page ranks for some irrelevant keywords but ranks higher for relevant keywords, you likely don’t need to worry.

For example, Coach’s contact page ranks in position 48 for “email coaching.” But it ranks in much higher positions for relevant keywords like “coach email”:

![Organic Search Positions – Coach email](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/12/organic-search-positions-coach-email.png "Organic Search Positions – Coach email")

This indicates that Google understands the main purpose of the page. And so Coach doesn’t need to take action in this case.

#### 3. It Can Dilute Your Topical Authority

Topical authority refers to your perceived expertise in a particular area.

Ranking for keywords outside of your niche can dilute your topical authority.

Why?

Because it makes your site seem less specialized.

Search engines may favor sites with topical authority—and so do users.

Consider this:

Would you rather get health advice from Website A or Website B?

|Website A Keywords|Website B Keywords|
|---|---|
|“healthy recipes”|“gut health”|
|“fitness plans”|“nutritional advice”|
|“paris travel guide”|“allergy symptoms”|
|“fashion trends”|“flu treatments”|
|“celebrity news”|“chronic pain”|

Website B, right?

The site has more topical authority because all of its keywords relate to health. Its health-related content appears to be more of a focus, and therefore more credible.

The site likely ranks higher for health-related keywords and gets better engagement from users than Website A.

### How to Check for Irrelevant Keyword Rankings

Here’s how to check if your website ranks for irrelevant keywords in Google, whether for organic (unpaid) results or paid ads.

#### How to Check for Irrelevant Organic Rankings

Organic rankings are search engine rankings you don’t pay for.

To check them, enter your domain into a tool like Semrush’s [Organic Research](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/organic/ "Organic Research: Keyword Rank Checker"). Then, choose your target country and click “**Search**.”

![Organic Research – Search – Coach](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/12/organic-research-search-coach.png "Organic Research – Search – Coach")

The “**Positions**” report lists the keywords you rank for, according to Semrush’s keyword database.

For each keyword, see which URL on your site ranks highest.

![Organic Research – Coach – Organic Search Positions](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/12/organic-research-coach-organic-search-positions.png "Organic Research – Coach – Organic Search Positions")

Scroll through to look for irrelevant keywords.

If you’re not sure whether a keyword is relevant, click the icon in the “SERP” column. This lets you view the [search engine results page (SERP)](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/serps "What Are SERPs & Why Are They Important for SEO?") for yourself.

![Organic Search Positions – View SERP](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/12/organic-search-positions-view-serp.png "Organic Search Positions – View SERP")

View the top-ranking results to see the search intent behind the keyword and whether it matches the purpose of your page.

For example, the SERP for “email coaching” is all about coaching clients via email:

![Google SERP – Email coaching](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/12/google-serp-email-coaching.png "Google SERP – Email coaching")

It wouldn’t make sense to target this keyword on Coach’s contact page.

If you find irrelevant keyword rankings worth addressing, you’ll need to remove or update your content. We’ll explain how later.

#### How to Check for Irrelevant Google Ads Rankings

If you use [Google Ads](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-advertising/ "What Is Google Ads & How Does It Work? A Comprehensive Guide"), make sure your ads don’t appear for irrelevant keywords to avoid wasting your PPC budget.

To start, [sign into Google Ads](https://ads.google.com/ "Google Ads").

Then, click “**Campaigns**” > “**Insights and reports**” > “**Search terms**.”

![Google Ads – Search terms](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/07/google-ads-search-terms.png "Google Ads – Search terms")

You’ll see a list of search terms that have triggered your ads.

Those with the lowest clicks and conversions are most likely irrelevant keywords.

![](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/06/icon-tip.png)

> **Tip:** If you’re not sure whether a keyword is relevant, search the keyword in Google to see what kinds of organic results it triggers.

If you find irrelevant keywords, update your ad campaign by considering the following:

- **[Keyword match types](https://backlinko.com/keyword-match-types "How to Use Keyword Match Types in Google Ads"):** This is how closely the user’s query must match your chosen keyword. Increase the specificity of the match type to reduce the risk of irrelevant keywords triggering your ads. But note that this may also lead to useful keywords being excluded.
- **[Negative keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/negative-keywords/ "Negative Keywords: What They Are & How to Use Them"):** These are terms that prevent your ads from showing. Again, there are different match types that affect the scope of excluded keywords.

### How to Avoid Irrelevant Keyword Rankings

#### Understand the Buyer’s Journey

The [buyer’s journey](https://www.semrush.com/blog/buyers-journey/ "The Buyer’s Journey: What It Is & How to Map It") refers to the steps someone goes through before making a purchase.

Understanding it helps you identify relevant keywords for your business.

![The Buyer's Journey](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/12/the-buyers-journey.png "The Buyer’s Journey")

Here’s an example of a buying journey for a mattress retailer:

1. The person experiences poor-quality sleep and looks for help online
2. They read a guide and discover that a new mattress could benefit them
3. They read mattress reviews and ultimately purchase a mattress

This means keywords like “why can’t I sleep” and “mattress reviews” are relevant to a mattress retailer.

But someone who searches for “quotes about sleep” is unlikely to be on a journey toward a mattress purchase. So, this is a largely irrelevant keyword for a mattress retailer.

#### Conduct Keyword Research

Keyword research is the process of finding keywords that can drive meaningful results for your business.

It provides crucial direction to your [search engine optimization (SEO)](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/what-is-seo "What is SEO? Search Engine Optimization In Plain English") and PPC efforts.

To streamline your keyword research, you can use a tool like Semrush’s [Keyword Magic Tool](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/ "Keyword Generator by Semrush").

Just enter your domain, choose your target country, and click “**Search**.” (You can also enter your domain for some personalized data.)

![Keyword Magic Tools – Search – Dog toys – Chewy](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/12/keyword-magic-tool-search-dog-toys-chewy.png "Keyword Magic Tools  – Search – Dog toys – Chewy")

You’ll see a list of “Broad Match” keywords that contain your starting keyword or a close variation.

The “Intent” column tells you the type(s) of search intent behind each keyword:

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|I|Informational|The user wants to find information|
|N|Navigational|The user wants to find a specific website or page|
|C|Commercial|The user wants to research a brand, product, or service|
|T|Transactional|The user wants to take action (e.g., buy something)|

![Keyword Magic Tools – Dog toys – Intent](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/12/keyword-magic-tools-dog-toys-intent.png "Keyword Magic Tools – Dog toys – Intent")

To better understand the search intent, click through to the SERP to view the top-ranking results.

Also pay attention to these keyword metrics:

- **Volume:** The average number of monthly searches for the keyword. The higher the number, the higher your result’s potential reach.
- **PKD %:** Your personal keyword difficulty score, expressed as a percentage. This measures how hard it’ll be for your domain to rank in the top 10 organic results.
- **Com.:** The competitive density score, which measures how hard it’ll be to secure an advertising space on the results page. It’s scored from 0 to 1.
- **CPC (USD):** The estimated cost per click (in U.S. dollars) on this results page. This is the amount advertisers pay each time someone clicks their ad for this keyword.

Save relevant keywords by using the checkboxes and the “**Send keywords**” button.

![Keyword Magic Tools – Dog toys – Send keywords](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/12/keyword-magic-tools-dog-toys-send-keywords.png "Keyword Magic Tools – Dog toys – Send keywords")

Now you’re ready to optimize your content or ad campaigns around your chosen keywords.

#### Focus on Content Quality

The higher your content quality, the more likely it is that Google will understand it and rank it for relevant (rather than irrelevant) keywords.

[Quality content](https://backlinko.com/quality-seo-content "Quality SEO Content: The QRIES Approach for Better Rankings") is generally:

- Created with a specific audience need in mind
- Well written (i.e., easy to read and free of errors)
- Comprehensive
- Accurate and trustworthy
- Structured with paragraphs and subheadings
- Enhanced with images, videos, and other visuals

For example, there are many signals that the page below is about sunflower seeds for planting—not eating:

- The site has topical authority on the subject of gardening
- Written content refers to the process of planting seeds and growing sunflowers
- Multiple photos of sunflowers (rather than seeds)
- Filters for “Flower color,” “Plant life cycle,” “Blooming season,” etc.

![ParkSeed – Sunflowers](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2024/12/parkseed-sunflowers.png "ParkSeed – Sunflowers")

All these factors help ensure that the page ranks prominently for relevant keywords like “sunflower seeds for planting.” And lower for irrelevant keywords like “edible sunflower seeds.”

#### Use Keyword Optimization Tactics

[Keyword optimization](https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-optimization/ "Keyword Optimization: 7 Steps to Getting It Right") involves strategically (but naturally) using keywords to help Google understand what your content is about.

Generally, every page should have a primary keyword that represents its overall topic. Use this keyword in the following locations:

- **[Title tag](https://www.semrush.com/blog/title-tag/ "What Is a Title Tag? How to Optimize Your SEO Titles"):** The page title that can appear in the search results page
- **[Meta description](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/meta-descriptions "How to Write SEO-Friendly Meta Descriptions"):** The page summary that can appear in the search results page
- **[H1 tag](https://backlinko.com/h1-tag "H1 Tags: Best Practices, Examples & Tips for SEO"):** The main heading that appears on the page
- **[URL slug](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/url-slug "What Is a URL Slug? (Why It Matters for SEO + Best Practices)"):** The unique part of the webpage’s address

You can also use the primary keyword (and any other relevant keywords, known as secondary keywords) in:

- Paragraphs
- Subheadings
- [Alt text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/alt-text/ "Alt Text: O que é e por que é importante para acessibilidade e SEO") (image descriptions used for accessibility purposes)

Also use relevant keywords when linking to a page internally.

In other words, if you’re linking to your men’s jeans product line, attach the link to [anchor text](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-is-anchor-text-and-how-can-i-optimize-it/ "O que é texto âncora? Uma visão geral + melhores práticas") like “men’s jeans” rather than “click here.”

This helps users and Google understand what the linked page is about.

**Apenas certifique-se de não exagerar.** Usar palavras-chave de forma não natural pode fazer mais mal do que bem, já que o preenchimento de [palavras-chave](https://backlinko.com/keyword-stuffing) é contra as [políticas de spam do Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies "Políticas de spam para Google Web Search | Google Search Central | Documentação").

#### Configure suas campanhas publicitárias com cuidado

Ao configurar uma campanha de pesquisa paga, escolha sua(s) palavra(s) a serem segmentadas e decida o tipo de correspondência de palavra-chave:[](https://www.semrush.com/blog/paid-search-ads/ "Um guia para anúncios de pesquisa pagos: definição + como configurar campanhas")

- **A correspondência ampla significa** que consultas de pesquisa **relacionadas à** sua palavra-chave podem acionar seu anúncio
- **A correspondência de frases significa** que consultas de pesquisa**, incluindo** o significado da sua palavra-chave, podem acionar seu anúncio
- **Correspondência exata significa que** as consultas de pesquisa **devem corresponder ao** significado da sua palavra-chave para acionar seu anúncio

The more specific the match type, the lower the chance of an irrelevant keyword triggering your ad. But, as we mentioned earlier, it could also exclude some relevant keywords.

Also consider using negative keywords. These are keywords that prevent your ads being triggered. And there are three negative keyword match types:

- **Negative broad match** means your ad won’t trigger if the query contains your negative keyword **in any order**
- **Negative phrase match** means your ad won’t trigger if the query contains your negative keyword **in the specified order**
- **Negative exact match** means your ad won’t trigger if the query is **exactly the same** as your negative keyword
