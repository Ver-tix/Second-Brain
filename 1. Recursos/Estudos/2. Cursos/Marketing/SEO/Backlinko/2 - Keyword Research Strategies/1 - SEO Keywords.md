### O que são palavras-chave de SEO?

Palavras-chave de SEO (também conhecidas como “palavras-chave” ou “frases-chave”) são termos adicionados ao conteúdo online para melhorar a classificação desses termos nos mecanismos de busca. A maioria das palavras-chave é descoberta durante o processo de pesquisa de [palavras-chave](https://backlinko.com/tools/keyword "Keyword Research") e é escolhida com base em uma combinação de volume de pesquisa, concorrência e [intenção comercial](https://backlinko.com/commercial-intent).

### Por que as palavras-chave de SEO são importantes?

Quando você otimiza [seu conteúdo](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/optimizing-content "Optimizing Content") em torno de palavras e frases que as pessoas pesquisam, seu site pode ter uma classificação mais alta para esses termos.

![Google still scans key areas of your site](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/google-still-scans-key-areas-of-your-site-1.png "Google still scans key areas of your site")

Nem é preciso dizer que uma classificação mais alta [nas SERPs](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/serps "What Are SERPs?") pode [gerar mais tráfego direcionado para seu site](https://backlinko.com/increase-website-traffic "Increase Website Traffic"). É por isso que encontrar palavras-chave que as pessoas pesquisam é o primeiro passo de qualquer campanha de [SEO](https://backlinko.com/seo-campaign "SEO campaign").

Na verdade, SEO é praticamente impossível sem palavras-chave.

Mas quando você tem uma lista das palavras-chave certas, você pode começar a trabalhar em tarefas importantes de SEO como:

- Descobrindo a arquitetura do [seu site](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/architecture "Site architecture")
- Planejando páginas de produtos e categorias
- Escrevendo conteúdo para postagens de blog e vídeos do YouTube
- Otimizando landing pages e páginas de vendas

Com isso, veja como encontrar palavras-chave para SEO.

### Best Practices

Use Google Suggest

Many people struggle with SEO because they target keywords that are WAY too competitive.

In fact, I’ve had many clients tell me that they want to rank for super competitive search queries like “weight loss” and “insurance”.

Is it possible to rank for single-word [SEO keywords](https://www.semrush.com/blog/what-are-keywords-simple-keyword-definition/ "What are Keywords?") like that? Sure.

But even if you do everything right, it can take years. Which is why I recommend focusing on [long tail keywords](https://backlinko.com/long-tail-keywords "Long Tail Keywords").

![Long tail keywords](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/long-tail-keywords.png "Long tail keywords")

Long tail keywords are long (usually 4+ word) terms that searchers use in Google and other search engines. And they tend to have a lower keyword difficulty compared to 1-3 word “head terms”.

So for people who are new to SEO, [long tails](https://trafficthinktank.com/long-tail-keywords/) are usually the best keywords to start with.

Felizmente, encontrar termos de cauda longa é muito fácil graças ao Google Suggest (também conhecido como Google Autocomplete).

Por exemplo, digamos que você queira criar uma página sobre “almoço cetogênico”. Bem, se o seu site é novo, a palavra-chave “almoço cetogênico” provavelmente é muito competitiva.

Mas quando você digita almoço cetogênico no Google, você obtém uma lista de sugestões de palavras-chave de cauda longa.

![Long tail keyword suggestions for "keto lunch"](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/long-tail-keyword-suggestions-for-keto-lunch.png "Long tail keyword suggestions for “keto lunch”")

Muito fofo.

Encontre palavras-chave baseadas em perguntas com estas duas ferramentas de palavras-chave gratuitas

Você está procurando palavras-chave para postagens e artigos de blog?

Se sim, então você vai adorar essas duas ferramentas gratuitas de [palavras-chave](https://backlinko.com/keyword-research-tools "Keyword Research Tools"). Ambas as ferramentas geram palavras-chave de perguntas. Quais são as palavras-chave PERFEITAS para criar conteúdo útil [de SEO](https://backlinko.com/seo-content "SEO content").[](https://backlinko.com/keyword-research-tools "Keyword Research Tools")[](https://backlinko.com/seo-content "SEO content")

A primeira ferramenta é chamada [AnswerThePublic.com](https://answerthepublic.com/ "Answer The Public").

![Answer The Public tool](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/answer-the-public-tool.png "Answer The Public tool")

This tool scans the web for questions that your target audience asks about your topic online.

![Answer The Public – 'link building' results](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/answer-the-public-link-building-results.png "Answer The Public – ‘link building’ results")

The second tool for finding question keywords is called “Question DB”.

![QuestionDB](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/question-db.png "QuestionDB")

It’s actually super similar to AnswerThePublic.com. But it tends to bring up its own unique set of questions.

![QuestionDB – 'link building' results](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/question-db-link-building-results.png "QuestionDB – ‘link building’ results")

The great thing about these tools is that the question keywords you get are long tail keywords. This means they usually don’t have a ton of competition on Google’s first page.

Find Outside The Box Keywords

The #1 issue with any keyword research tool is that they usually spit out words and phrases that are super related to the seed keyword you typed into it.

For example, look at the keyword suggestions that I get from [Semrush](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/semrush "Semrush") when I type in “[SEO tips](https://backlinko.com/actionable-seo-tips)”.

![Semrush – Keyword Variations – SEO tips](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2022/02/semrush-keyword-variations-seo-tips.png "Semrush – Keyword Variations – SEO tips")

These are decent keywords. But they’re not super creative.

If you want to find creative keywords, I recommend checking out a nifty [free SEO tool](https://backlinko.com/best-free-seo-tools "Best Free SEO Tools") called [SeedKeywords.com](https://www.seedkeywords.com/ "SeedKeywords.com").

This tool lets you ask customers, colleagues or clients how THEY would search for something in Google.

![SeedKeywords example](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/seed-keywords-example.png "SeedKeywords example")

And, if you’re like me, you’ll probably find a handful of terms that you wouldn’t have been able to think of on your own (or get from any tool).

![Resultados do SeedKeywords](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/seed-keywords-results.png "Resultados do SeedKeywords")

Use Amazon to Find [Ecommerce Keywords](https://backlinko.com/tools/ecommerce-keywords)

Keyword research for eCommerce is similar to keyword research for a blog.

The one big difference is that, [with ecommerce SEO](https://backlinko.com/ecommerce-seo "SEO para comércio eletrônico"), you need to find keywords for product and category pages… and lots of them.

Fortunately, you can find lots of excellent product keywords using Amazon Suggest. This works the exact same way as Google Suggest.

You just type in a product or product category into Amazon…

![Pesquisa de produtos da Amazon retorna palavras-chave de cauda longa](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/amazon-product-search-returns-long-tail-keywords.png "Pesquisa de produtos da Amazon retorna palavras-chave de cauda longa")

…and jot down any terms that come up.

Find Terms That Competitors Already Rank For

Typing a bunch of random keywords into a tool can only get you so far.

In fact, I find myself using traditional keyword tools less and less. Instead, I just look at the keywords that my competitors already rank for.

Not only does this generate a laundry list of keywords, but it’s SUPER fast. I just pop in a competing domain… and I get a list of thousands of keywords within seconds.

![SEMrush – Resultados de palavras-chave para Backlinko](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/sem-rush-keyword-results-for-backlinko.png "SEMrush – Resultados de palavras-chave para Backlinko")

There are [a number of SEO tools](https://backlinko.com/seo-tools "Ferramentas de SEO") that reverse engineer your competitor’s best SEO keywords. Most, if not all of them, are paid.

But if you already use [Semrush](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/semrush "Semrush"), you already have this feature at your disposal.

Pro Tip: Reverse engineer websites in your league. For example, if your site doesn’t have a ton of Domain Authority, don’t reverse engineer the #1 site in your space. Instead, use a website that’s similar to yours. That way, you’ll find keywords that you have a decent chance of ranking for.

Find Search Volume and CPC Data With Google’s Keyword Tool

[The Google Keyword Planner](https://backlinko.com/google-keyword-planner "O planejador de palavras-chave do Google") is super helpful because the data comes from the most reliable source of keyword data: Google itself.

However, the Keyword Planner has two major issues.

1. You need to set up a Google Ads account to use it
2. Unless you’re running a campaign, you get monthly keyword search volume ranges

Fortunately, you can get around both of these issues pretty easily.

First, even though you need a Google Ads account to use the Keyword Planner, that doesn’t mean that you need to run any ads. As long as your account is fully set up, you’re good to go.

For example, we’re not currently running any ads and still have full access to the tool.

![Planejador de palavras-chave do Google](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/google-keyword-planner-e1567012381919.png "Planejador de palavras-chave do Google")

And if the tool is giving you a search volume range, like this…

![Planejador de palavras-chave do Google – Volume de pesquisa](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/google-keyword-planner-search-volume.png "Planejador de palavras-chave do Google – Volume de pesquisa")

You can add a keyword that you’re interested into a plan.

![Planejador de palavras-chave do Google – Adicionar ao plano](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/google-keyword-planner-add-to-plan.png "Planejador de palavras-chave do Google – Adicionar ao plano")

And the Plan Overview will show you the exact impressions you’ll get (which is basically monthly search volume).

Discover Popular Keywords With Google Trends

Yes, monthly search volume is important.

After all, there’s no point in ranking for a keyword that no one searches for.

O problema é que o volume de pesquisa não informa como essa palavra-chave está se tornando tendência. E, a longo prazo, a tendência de uma palavra-chave é mais importante do que seu volume bruto de pesquisa.

Felizmente, você pode ver facilmente a tendência de uma palavra-chave usando o Google [Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/ "Tendências do Google").

![Pesquisa do Google Trends](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/google-trends-search.png "Pesquisa do Google Trends")

Por exemplo, há algum tempo publiquei [um guia para o Google Search Console](https://backlinko.com/google-search-console "Console de pesquisa do Google").

![Backlinko – Console de Pesquisa do Google](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2021/01/backlinko-google-search-console-2021.png "Backlinko – Console de Pesquisa do Google")

Por que escolhi esse tópico e palavra-chave?

Não porque muitas pessoas estivessem procurando por isso. Na verdade, a maioria das pessoas ainda o chamava pelo seu antigo nome: Google Webmaster Tools.

Mas quando olhei no Google Trends, vi que uma palavra-chave estava explodindo… e a outra estava morrendo lentamente.

![Comparação do Google Trends](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/google-trends-comparison.png "Comparação do Google Trends")

Este é um exemplo extremo. Mas mostra que o volume de pesquisa mensal que uma ferramenta mostra é um instantâneo da popularidade dessa palavra-chave. Ele não informa se essa palavra-chave está explodindo… ou desaparecendo.

Pessoalmente, me esforço MUITO em cada conteúdo que publico. E para que esse esforço valha a pena, meu conteúdo precisa atrair tráfego direcionado durante anos. E para fazer isso, concentro-me em termos que estão crescendo em popularidade.

Double Down on SEO Keywords That You Already Rank For

When it comes to keyword research for SEO, most people focus 100% on finding new keywords.

And hey, there’s nothing wrong with that.

But to get a FULL list of keywords that you can optimize around, you also want to look at the keywords that you currently rank for.

And the best way to do that?

The Google Search Console.

To execute this strategy, head over to the Performance section.

![Google Search Console – Desempenho](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/google-search-console-performance.png "Google Search Console – Desempenho")

Then, look at specific keywords that you rank for in the organic search results. You’ll probably already recognize 90% of these keywords.

But if you dig deep, you might also come across a handful of keywords that surprise you.

For example, according to the Search Console, I rank for “youtube description example”.

![Google Search Console – "exemplo de descrição do YouTube"](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/google-search-console-youtube-description-example.png "Google Search Console – “exemplo de descrição do youtube”")

The page that ranks for this term includes an example.

![Exemplo de descrição de vídeo](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/video-description-example.png "Exemplo de descrição de vídeo")

But it’s really not optimized around that term.

Well, now that I know that Google likes my site as a result for that keyword I can create a new web page that’s 100% optimized around “YouTube description example”.

And that page has a great chance of ranking high on the first page for that term.

How to Use SEO Keywords In Your Content

Now that you’ve found a bunch of SEO marketing keywords, the next step is to use those keywords in your content.

Here’s exactly how to set that up:

First, decide on one main keyword.

In other words: don’t optimize your content around several different search terms. It very rarely works.

For example, [my guide to writing a blog post](https://backlinko.com/write-a-blog-post "Escreva uma postagem no blog") is optimized around “how to write a blog post”.

![Backlinko – Escreva uma postagem no blog](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2020/06/backlinko-write-a-blog-post.png "Backlinko – Escreva uma postagem no blog")

Embora eu use variações dessa palavra-chave (como “escrever uma postagem de blog”) no meu conteúdo, meu [SEO na página](https://backlinko.com/on-page-seo "SEO na página") está praticamente 100% focado na minha palavra-chave principal.

Em seguida, use sua palavra-chave principal algumas vezes em seu conteúdo. Não há necessidade de exagerar e repetir sua palavra-chave 100 vezes. Basta mencionar sua palavra-chave algumas vezes. Dessa forma, o Google sabe que sua página é sobre essa palavra-chave.

![Use sua palavra-chave alvo em seu conteúdo](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/use-your-target-keyword-in-your-content.png "Use sua palavra-chave alvo em seu conteúdo")

Por exemplo, esta página usa minha palavra-chave de destino algumas vezes na página.

![Dicas de SEO Palavra-chave na postagem](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2020/06/seo-tips-keyword-in-post.png "Dicas de SEO Palavra-chave na postagem")

Em seguida, certifique-se de que sua palavra-chave apareça na tag de título e no URL.

Por exemplo, minha principal palavra-chave para [este post](https://backlinko.com/seo-techniques "Actionable SEO Tips") é “técnicas de SEO”.

![SEO techniques post keyword](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2021/02/seo-techniques-post-keyword.png "SEO techniques post keyword")

E certifiquei-me de usar essa palavra-chave (uma vez) na minha tag de título e no meu URL.

!["SEO techniques" keyword in title and URL](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2021/02/seo-techniques-keyword-in-title-and-url.png "“SEO techniques” keyword in title and URL")

Em seguida, adicione sua palavra-chave à sua meta descrição.

Does Google care about the terms that show up in your meta description for SEO? [Nope](https://webmasters.googleblog.com/2009/09/google-does-not-use-keywords-meta-tag.html "Google doesn't care about the terms that show up in your meta description for SEO")!

![Google does not use the keywords meta tag](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/google-does-not-use-the-keywords-meta-tag.png "Google does not use the keywords meta tag")

You want to add your keyword to your description because it helps your snippet stand out in the organic search results.

![Bold keywords in your meta description can increase CTR](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/bold-keywords-in-your-meta-description-can-increase-ctr.png "Bold keywords in your meta description can increase CTR")

For example, check out my meta description for this page from my blog.

![Meta description in Google SERP](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2021/02/meta-description-in-google-serp.png "Meta description in Google SERP")

See how Google bolds the keyword?

That really helps my result stand out and improves my [organic click-through-rate](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/organic-ctr "Organic CTR").

Next, sprinkle in a few variations of your main keyword.

For example, for my page optimized for “SEO techniques”, I added variations of that term like “SEO tips” and “search engine optimization” on the page.

![Variations of keyword example](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/variations-of-keyword-example.png "Variations of keyword example")

You can easily find relevant keyword variations in the “Searches Related to” section at the bottom of the search results.

![Searches related to 'compost'](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/searches-related-to-compost.png "Searches related to ‘compost’")

Finally, [use internal links](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/internal-links "Internal links") to send authority to your new page.

The fact is: new pages don’t have any [backlinks](https://backlinko.com/backlinks-guide "Backlinks Guide") or authority. But when you internally link to that new page, it gets INSTANT link authority.

![Link to important pages](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/link-to-important-pages.png "Link to important pages")

One more thing to keep in mind with internal links: make sure to use anchor text that includes your target keyword. That helps Google understand that your page is about that specific topic.

![Use descriptive anchor text to link pages](https://api.backlinko.com/app/uploads/2019/08/use-descriptive-anchor-text-to-link-pages.png "Use descriptive anchor text to link pages")
